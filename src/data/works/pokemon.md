I've had a fondness for Pokémon since childhood and can confidently name most Pokémon up to the 7th generation. That's what drove me to make this simple web app: to test the limits of my existing knowledge of Pokémon and learn about new ones I definitely missed.

The idea kicked in right around when I was starting to become interested in web development. I wanted to learn React, and I figured this would be a good starting project for that. The website is admittedly simple, as I shelved it for more pressing projects, and could benefit from some polishing and additional features.

Pokémon information is retrieved from the publicly-available [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co/), a free and open-source RESTful API.
